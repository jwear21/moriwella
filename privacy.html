Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Moriwella is a private place to remember your life, organize your days, and live well. It is made by JSW Software Designs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name, email address and a password. We store the password only in scrambled form, so we cannot read it.
  • What you write and save. Journal entries, experiences, photos you add, lists, goals, trackers, and the Memory Movies you make.
  • Household details. If you share a plan, the names and emails of the people you invite, and which lists you share with them.
  • Billing records. Which plan you have, your credits, and your payment history. Your card number goes straight to our payment provider, Stripe, and never reaches our servers.
  • Basic technical data. Your sign-in session, your language and display choices, and short-lived security logs such as failed sign-in attempts.

How we use it

  • To run Moriwella: show your content to you and to the people you choose to share it with.
  • To take payments, give you credits, and send receipts.
  • To email you about your account: invitations, password links, billing notices, and replies to your questions.
  • To keep the service safe and fix problems.

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not use your journal or photos to train AI models, and we do not read your entries. We may look at your account only when you ask us for help, or when needed to protect the service or follow the law.

Who can see your content

Your entries and photos are private to you. Lists and experiences are visible only to the people you add to them. A household owner can see who is in the household and manage seats, but cannot read another member's private journal.

Who we share data with

  • Stripe processes payments and subscriptions.
  • Our hosting provider, ShockHosting, stores the service and its data on servers in the United States.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We do not share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your content while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your content within 30 days. We keep payment and tax records as the law requires. Copies in server backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

Your choices

  • Change your name, picture and password in Settings at any time.
  • Delete your account from Settings, or on our account deletion page.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or a correction, by emailing privacy@moriwella.com.

If you live where privacy laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act apply, these rights include access, correction, deletion and objection. Email us and we will respond within the time the law allows.

Security

Moriwella is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Passwords are stored scrambled, and access to our systems is limited. No service can promise perfect security, but we take it seriously and will tell you promptly if something affects your data.

Children

Moriwella is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

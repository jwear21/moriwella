Interaction kit

Working demos of the milestone celebrations and the page-turning book that the Moriwella app will use. Prototype only.

Milestone celebrations

Tap a cup to log 8 oz. Everyday check-offs get a small sparkle. Reach 64 oz and you get fireworks and a badge.

0 of 64 oz

Page turning

Swipe, use the arrow keys, or press the buttons. On a phone it shows one page at a time.

Plans, trials and credits

Referral program

You can invite friends to Moriwella with a personal link from the Invite friends page. By using your link and agreeing to these rules in the app, you accept them.

  • Who can take part. Any account holder whose account is open and in good standing.
  • What your friend gets. A friend who is new to Moriwella and signs up through your link can choose a plan and add a credit card. Their first 30 days of that plan are included and nothing is charged during that time. They can cancel before the 30 days end and pay nothing. After that the plan renews monthly at the price shown, until they cancel. Someone who already has an account, or has already used a free trial, does not qualify.
  • What you get. Once your friend's first payment has been received, we add one month of your own plan to your account as a credit on your next bill. If your plan is a yearly plan, the credit is one-twelfth of the yearly price. If you are on a free plan, a trial, or have no bill to credit, you receive 3 movie credits instead. There is no limit on how many friends you can refer, and each friend counts once.
  • Rewards are not cash. A reward has no cash value, cannot be transferred or sold, and is not refundable.
  • When a reward is taken back. If your friend's first payment is refunded, disputed or charged back, we remove the reward. If it has already been applied, we take it back from your account.
  • Playing fair. Do not refer yourself, create extra accounts for yourself or members of your own household, or invite people by spam or unwanted bulk messages. Do not mislead people about what Moriwella costs or what the offer is. Follow the laws that apply to messages and advertising where you live and where your friends live.

  • Privacy. You will see your friend's name and how far along they are. You will not see their journal, payments or any other details.
  • Changes. We may change or end the program at any time. A change does not affect rewards already earned.

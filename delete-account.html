Delete your Moriwella account

Last updated November 20, 2026

You can ask us to permanently delete your Moriwella account and the content in it at any time.

In the app

  1. Sign in and open Settings.
  2. Open Delete my account.
  3. Enter your password and choose Request account deletion.

By email

If you cannot sign in, write to support@moriwella.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm it is you before we start.

What happens

  • We delete your account and its content within 30 days: your entries, photos, lists you own, goals, trackers, Memory Movies and settings. We email you when it is done.
  • If you pay for Moriwella, we stop your billing.
  • We keep payment and tax records for as long as the law requires. They are not connected to your writing.
  • Copies in server backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.
  • If you own a household, the people you invited keep their own accounts, but the shared plan ends.

Questions? Email privacy@moriwella.com. See also our privacy policy.
